Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our transit routes, schedules, and ticketing policies.

What is your ticket cancellation and refund policy? 🚗

Cancellations are accepted up to 24 hours before departure. Tickets must be unused and requested through your account portal.

How long do transit schedules take to update? 🚆

Standard route schedules update in real-time or within 3-7 business days for seasonal timetable changes. Faster notices are posted at terminal checkouts.

Do you offer international or cross-border travel options? ✈️

Yes, to many neighboring regions. Costs and transit times vary and are clearly shown during checkout.

How can I track my trip or vehicle status? 📍

You will receive a live GPS tracking link by email once your trip is active. It is also fully visible in your transit account profile.

How do I contact customer support? 📞

Email groupwais@gmail.com or use the online contact form. Our transit team replies within 1 business day.

Are luggage and bicycles allowed on board? 🧳

Yes, standard luggage is free of charge. Bicycles are allowed in designated storage areas with a small reservation fee.

Do you offer discounted passes for students or seniors? 🎫

Yes, we offer reduced weekly and monthly pass rates for students, seniors, and frequent commuters upon verification.

What should I do if I leave an item behind in a vehicle? 📦

Please contact our terminal support immediately with your trip details and seat number so our team can check the lost and found inventory.
